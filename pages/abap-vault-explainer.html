ABAP Knowledge Vault

A library with a robot librarian

Drop a document in the mail slot. Everything after that is automatic: the knowledge inside is read, sorted onto the right shelf, and catalogued — with a full paper trail.

1

The mail slot

Someone drops a document in

The folder raw/inbox/ is the only door into the vault. Anyone on the team can drop raw material here — meeting notes, Word or PDF documents, slide decks, spreadsheets, call transcripts, even a photo of a whiteboard. No formatting required.

2

The alarm bell

The system notices new mail

A small instruction file — .github/workflows/abap-vault-ingest.yml — does no thinking of its own. It simply watches the mail slot and rings the bell the moment something arrives. It also rings once every Monday morning as a safety sweep, in case anything was missed, and it saves all finished work back to the shared vault.

3

The librarian

An AI reads it and files the knowledge

The worker — .github/scripts/abap-ingest.py — picks up each document and, with the help of Claude (the AI), turns raw material into organized knowledge:

a.Checks “have I read this before?” so nothing is processed twice
b.Converts any format — Word, PDF, PowerPoint, Excel, transcripts, images — into readable text
c.Asks the AI: “what in here is worth keeping permanently?” — decisions, lessons, who owns what
d.Writes or updates the wiki pages, updates the catalog, and records what it did
e.Moves the original into the archive — nothing is ever thrown away
4

The shelves

Knowledge lands on one of four shelves

Standards

01-standards/

The rulebooks: coding standards, architecture principles, system landscape. Rarely changes.

Workstreams

02-workstreams/

The active project work: people, meetings, decisions, specs, what's being built, open questions.

Intelligence

03-intelligence/

The lessons: things learned the hard way, written down so nobody pays for the same mistake twice.

Internal

04-internal/

The team operations: contacts, onboarding guides, step-by-step procedures.

5

The librarian's desk & the archive

Every action is recorded

The meta/ folder is the librarian's desk — the bookkeeping that keeps the library orderly. The raw/processed/ drawer keeps every original document forever.

The catalog meta/index.md
Table of contents for the whole vault — where you start when looking for anything
The diary meta/log.md
A permanent history of every document read and every page it changed
The “already read” list meta/inbox.md
Fingerprints of every processed file, so duplicates are skipped automatically
The name dictionary meta/entities.md
“O2C”, “OTC”, “Order-to-Cash” — one official name, so nothing is filed twice under different names
The archive raw/processed/
Every original document, kept forever — you can always check the source

The constitution: CLAUDE.md

One rulebook governs everything the librarian does — what counts as knowledge worth keeping, how pages must be named, where each type of page lives, and how everything links together. It's why the vault stays consistent no matter who dropped the file or what it looked like.

The one-sentence version: team members drop any document into a shared inbox; an AI librarian reads it, keeps what matters, files it into a well-organized wiki, and leaves a full paper trail — so the team's knowledge survives even when people move on.
