ABAP Knowledge Vault

Sample questions to ask the vault

Once the wiki is populated, you query it in plain English — no search syntax, no folder hunting. These are good first questions for a new team member, and a quick way to check the vault actually holds what it should.

Standards & conventions

1

What are the ABAP naming conventions for declaring structures, table types, variables, classes, and function modules?

2

What are the general ABAP development guidelines?

3

What is the recommended structure or template for an ABAP report header block?

Performance & tooling

4

What are the performance guidelines for SAP ECC systems?

5

What SAP analysis tools can developers use to evaluate the efficiency and performance of ABAP objects?

Language & syntax

6

What are the alternative ways to declare a work area in ABAP?

How to ask

Open a terminal in the wiki folder and start Claude Code, then paste the question. Claude reads the vault as its working context and answers from the pages — citing which ones it used.

If an answer comes back thin, that is the signal: the knowledge is not in the vault yet. Drop the source document into raw/inbox/ and ask again after the next ingest run.

Why this page exists: a vault is only as good as the answers it gives. Keep this list handy as a smoke test — run it after every big ingest and you will know immediately what the wiki still cannot answer. See also the vault explainer and the discovery questions.
